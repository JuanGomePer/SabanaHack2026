Dear Prof. Qu and co-authors,

We are a student team at Universidad de La Sabana (Colombia) developing, for the Sabana Hack 2026 challenge with AGRAR, an academic prototype for early visual detection of blueberry diseases.

Your article states that "the raw data supporting the conclusions of this article will be made available by the authors, without undue reservation." We would be grateful for access to the **927 original images** and their labels (infection site, stage, severity), for **non-commercial research only**:

- evaluating generalization of blueberry organ and disease classifiers;
- studying field backgrounds and severity annotation practices.

We will cite your work, will not redistribute the images, will keep them in private storage, and will follow any additional terms you set. Please also let us know which images came from third-party sources (e.g., Bugwood.org) so we can respect their terms.
